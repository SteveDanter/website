Choosing My Next Bike

While I still owned the Transalp, I began considering what might replace it. The shortlist changed several times—and my eventual choice wasn’t even on it.

Chapterbikes More in this chapter →

Which one would be next?

I started thinking about my next motorcycle almost a year before I was in a financial position to replace the Transalp. At that stage, it was little more than enjoyable speculation—but looking at possible new bikes is half the fun.

BMW F900XR

I initially went to test-ride the BMW F800GS and thought it was fantastic. When I returned to the showroom, however, I spotted the F900XR.

The XR had the upright riding position I had come to prefer, but appeared more suited to road riding than the GS and its softer, more adventure-oriented suspension. It seemed to offer an appealing combination of comfort, performance and road-going ability.

Unfortunately, the Transalp didn’t yet have enough trade-in value to make changing financially practical. I estimated that another ten months might put me in a position to become the proud owner of an XR.

BMW F800GS and F900GS

The F800GS remained a possibility after that enjoyable test ride, while the F900GS offered another—rather more expensive—route into BMW ownership. Both appealed to the adventure-bike rider in me, although I was beginning to wonder whether I really needed another bike designed with off-road ability that I was unlikely to use.

Voge DS900X

Then there was the Voge DS900X. It bore a striking resemblance to the BMW and appeared to offer an enormous amount of motorcycle for roughly two-thirds of the price.

Was I ready to buy a Chinese motorcycle? Possibly. The value for money certainly made it difficult to ignore.

Suzuki V-Strom 800

The Suzuki V-Strom 800 was another strong contender. I had thoroughly enjoyed owning my V-Strom 1050, so returning to the family made a lot of sense.

The smaller parallel-twin engine also appealed to me. If it proved anything like the engine in the Honda, it should be light, flexible and great fun to ride.

Decision made

I spent months considering the options, comparing specifications and changing my mind. There was the BMW I had ridden, the BMWs I admired, the tempting value of the Voge and the familiar appeal of another V-Strom.

Then, when the time finally came, I chose something that hadn’t appeared on my original shortlist at all.
