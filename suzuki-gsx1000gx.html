Suzuki GSX-S1000GX+ Review

Suzuki GSX-S1000GX+

So far I can't fault this bike, well apart from the overly close ratio sporty gearbox, but I think that is more to do with my riding style than the bike itself.

I chose this bike based on a test ride on the GSX-S1000GT, a more sporty looking bike but phyically smaller than a lot of it's rivals, they did have a GX in the showroom but it was already sold so I could sit on it but not test ride it, I prefered the GX due to it's more upright sitting position.

What I didn't concider though was the increased seat height which is expected as this is designed as a crossover bike, not quite sporty road bike but not quite rugged offroader either.

Once again I headed off to Sunny France for 12 nights in yet another luxury caravan, this time Aunis Club Vendee close to the town of Le Tranche-Sur-Mer in the Vendee region.

As is now usual, I took the overnight ferry from Portsmouth to Ouistreham(Caen) arriving in France at 8am, all ready for a nice leisurely ride south.

On my way there, disaster, I was taking the backroads, no rush to get there, following the SatNav but "correcting" it when it kept directing me away from the beautiful little towns and villages and onto the by-passes. Anyway it kept insisting I turn left so I gave it the benefit of the doubt in the town of Chemaze and turned left into a very short road that ended after 25m with a no entry road leading to houses, I stopped against the kerb ready to do a 360 degree turn and waited for the oncoming car to pass, the oncoming car somehow managed to miss the front of my bike but hit my left pannier, that pushed my bike over, me still sitting on it, onto the pavement, my right foot was trapped underneath, I pulled it out and a very nice man with his three kids came over an lifted my bike back up and onto its stand.

I think I had a broken a toe or two, they walked, I hobbled, back to the lady's house and did all the insurance paperwork, the man that helped right the bike came with me and helped with the language barrier, anyway, one cup of tea and lots of apologies later I was on my way again.

So I eventually got to the site and checked in, the pain in my foot was probably a 9/10 by now. I found that dangling my foot in the swimming pool and also drinking French lager helped with the pain control.

The following day I umm'd and ahh'd about going to a hospital to get it checked out but not being a wuss I went to the local Pharmacy in Le Tranche-Sur-Mer and picked up some pain killers and cream< and manned up and got on with it./p>

Kind of spoilt my walking holiday though...

Still moving.
