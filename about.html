About me (and my blog)…

What started as a little project to keep my brain active and teach myself a bit of web design somehow turned into something much bigger: part travel diary, part autobiography, part photo album and, increasingly, a place to store memories before I forget them.

Chapter life More in this chapter →

This site was only ever meant to be a small project for myself, something to keep my brain active while giving me an excuse to learn a little about web design.

The original idea was simple enough: build a website around one of my trips to France and use that as a way of learning how websites worked. The problem was that the more I tried to find things to write about France, the more I found myself wandering off down memory lane instead.

One old photograph would remind me of a bike. The bike would remind me of a job. The job would remind me of somebody I hadn't thought about for years, and before long my little French holiday website had turned into a rather vague story about my life — so far, anyway.

France did eventually get its own section, although, like most of this website, it remains very much a work in progress. That is probably part of the fun of it. There is always another photograph to find, another trip to remember and another story that suddenly comes back to me when I least expect it.

Rose-tinted glasses firmly attached

I make no claim that this is an entirely objective historical record. Like most people, I probably remember the good bits more clearly than the bad ones, and I suspect the rose-tinted glasses are permanently fixed to my face.

If I ever appear to blame, criticise or belittle anybody along the way, it is almost certainly unintentional. All characters appearing in these stories are entirely fictional and any resemblance to real people, living or dead — even when I name them directly — is purely coincidental. Honest.

If you happen to recognise yourself in one of the stories, please take it as a compliment to your vivid imagination.

A slightly unreliable timeline

Initially I tried to organise everything into a neat timeline based around the vehicles I've owned and the jobs I've had. It seemed like a good idea at the time.

Unfortunately, my memory doesn't always cooperate.

The result is a timeline that can be fairly fluid, with occasional overlaps, gaps and stories that may turn up more than once. Some events are crystal clear while others are little more than fragments tied to a photograph, a car, a motorbike or a place.

I am definitely neither a storyteller nor an author, and I make no apology for that. In my defence, some of the memories on these pages go back more than sixty years. The surprising thing is probably not how much I've forgotten, but how much still comes back once I start writing.

Where does the story begin?

I suppose the logical starting point is before cars and motorbikes entered the picture, when getting anywhere meant either using a bicycle or relying on my own two feet.

From there came bikes, cars, jobs, France, holidays, mistakes, adventures and all the other bits and pieces that somehow became my life.

So, with all that in mind, the remnants of the original French website are still here somewhere, but the focus has shifted rather dramatically.

It turns out the subject I know most about is me.

Me, me, me…
