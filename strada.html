Fiat Strada 65cl

My first car.

I bought this off my parents, a bargain, about £400.

In my mind this had the sportiest engine you could ever imagine, tyres so wide it was difficult to fit on a normal road, 145/65/15’s I think and handling that was second to none.

The only downside was that it didn’t have rear wheel drive so I couldn’t drift it and do donuts like the popular kids down Llandow runway.

So with that in mind I bought my second car

Still moving.
